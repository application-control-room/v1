How limits work

Know what scales with your plan.

01

Account capacity

Application and monthly event limits apply across the account according to the selected plan.

02

Per-app capacity

Uptime monitors, PM2 hosts, feature flags, alert rules and alert channels are listed as limits for each application.

03

Retention

Telemetry retention increases from 7 days on Free to 15 days on Pro and 30 days on Max.
