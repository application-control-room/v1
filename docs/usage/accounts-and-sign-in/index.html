Accounts and sign-in

An ACR account is just a person. It has no role of its own: what someone can do depends on the apps they are a member of. Registering creates the account only; you then create an app or accept an invitation.

Register​

  1. Open Create account (/auth/register).
  2. Enter full name, e-mail, password (twice) and solve the captcha.
  3. Verify the six-digit code sent to your e-mail (/auth/verify-email). You are signed in when it is accepted.
FieldRule
Password8–72 characters with at least one letter and one digit
PhoneNot on the form. Add an optional 10-digit Indian mobile number starting 6–9 later under Profile
E-mailMust be unique (case-insensitive)
CaptchaAn image with six characters. Letters and digits that look alike (0/O, 1/I) are left out

The verification code​

  • Six digits, valid for OTP_EXPIRY_MINUTES (10 by default).
  • Five wrong attempts burn the code; you must request a new one.
  • Resending is limited to three per ten minutes per e-mail address.

Sign in​

Use e-mail, password and captcha. After five failed attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes (423 ACCOUNT_LOCKED). Unverified accounts are asked to verify first. Sessions last up to 30 days and renew while you are active (14-day sliding refresh).

Continue with Google​

The Continue with Google button signs you in with a Google account:

  • A new Google account creates a user without a password. You can set one later under Profile.
  • If a verified account with the same e-mail exists, Google is linked to it and your password keeps working.
  • If an unverified account with that e-mail exists, Google is linked, the account becomes verified, and its password is replaced with an unusable one. Whoever registered it never proved they own the address.

Secure your account​

If your account does not have two-factor authentication yet, every sign-in (password, Google, or the first sign-in after verifying your email) opens a Secure your account page before the console. It explains what two-factor authentication protects against and sets it up in three steps: scan the QR code with your authenticator app, enter the 6-digit code, and save your backup codes. Remind me later takes you straight to the page you were going to; the page appears again at your next sign-in (not when you reload). Once two-factor authentication is on, it no longer appears, and sign-in asks for a code from your app. See Profile and sessions.

Forgot your password​

  1. Forgot password → enter your e-mail and captcha. The page always answers the same way, whether or not the address has an account.
  2. If an active, verified account exists you receive a six-digit code and a link to the reset page. The e-mail address is never put in the URL.
  3. Enter the code and a new password. All your sessions are signed out and you sign in again with the new password.

Why a "device" appears​

Before registering, verifying or signing in, the ACR console registers your browser as a device. A device token is stored in the browser's IndexedDB and lets ACR rate-limit abuse and tie sessions to a browser. If the browser blocks storage you see a banner explaining that sign-in cannot continue. You never type a device token.

Accepting an invitation​

Open the link from the invitation e-mail (/invite/<token>). If you do not have an account the page offers to create one with the invited address. After verifying you can accept; you land in the app with the invited role. See Members and roles.

Security behaviour at a glance​

ProtectionBehaviour
PasswordsStored only as a strong one-way hash, never in readable form
Account lockout5 failures, 15 minutes
Account privacySign-in and password-reset answers do not reveal whether an e-mail address has an account
Signed-in sessionShort-lived, renewed automatically while you work. Re-using a stolen sign-in token signs the session out
AuditSign-ins, failures, lockouts, resets and profile changes are recorded
