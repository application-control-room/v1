Alerts

Turn important changes into an incident—not more noise.

Alerts evaluates telemetry and uptime conditions, deduplicates open incidents, applies cooldowns and mute windows, and delivers notifications through encrypted channel targets.

Questions answered

Move from signal to decision.

Each alerts view is organised around a concrete operating question rather than an undifferentiated stream of telemetry.

01

What can trigger an alert?

Watch new or regressed issues, error spikes, inactive apps, p95 latency, Web Vitals, release crash-free health and uptime outages.

02

Where will it go?

Deliver to email, Slack, Discord, Teams or a generic webhook; webhook creation returns its signing secret once.

03

What happened afterward?

Track triggered or resolved state, acknowledgment and every channel delivery attempt or failure.

How it works

A clear path through the workflow.

ACR keeps collection, processing and action explicit so the team knows where every result comes from.

Important boundaryAlert targets are encrypted at rest. A rule is only useful when its thresholds and destinations have been tested against the app’s actual signal volume.

  1. 1

    Connect a channel

    Create and test a destination; full webhook targets remain masked after storage.

  2. 2

    Define a rule

    Choose a signal, threshold, evaluation window, cooldown and destination set.

  3. 3

    Work the incident

    Open, acknowledge and follow resolution with delivery status attached.
