Acceptable Use Policy

Draft rules for safe and lawful use of ACR.

Document statusDraft templateUpdated 8 November 2026
Provisional content

This draft is provided for product preparation only. It is not legal advice and must be reviewed by qualified counsel. Replace bracketed placeholders before publication.

1. Lawful use

You may use ACR only in compliance with applicable law, contracts, privacy notices and the rights of other people.

2. Prohibited activity

You must not use ACR to:

  • Send malware, exploit systems, scan for vulnerabilities without authorization or interfere with service availability.
  • Collect telemetry from a person or device without an appropriate lawful basis, notice or consent.
  • Submit secrets, payment-card data, government identifiers, health data or other sensitive data contrary to an approved agreement.
  • Attempt to bypass role checks, rate limits, allowed origins, credentials or tenant isolation.
  • Use flags or announcements to distribute malicious code, deceptive content or unlawful material.
  • Resell, benchmark publicly, reverse engineer or access the service beyond rights granted in an approved agreement.

3. Credential and integration hygiene

Use client keys only in browsers, keep server secrets in a secret manager, restrict allowed origins, use opaque user IDs and avoid logging secrets. Promptly rotate or revoke exposed credentials.

4. Resource abuse

Do not deliberately exceed technical limits, generate artificial telemetry load or circumvent retention and query restrictions. Published technical defaults are safety controls and may not be commercial quotas.

5. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations and restrict or suspend access when reasonably necessary to protect the service or others. Final notice, appeal, preservation and termination procedures must be aligned with approved Terms.

6. Reporting

Report abuse to [abuse contact email]. Security vulnerabilities should use the process in the Security Policy.
